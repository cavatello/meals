The month

The 30-day plan.

Each day runs from breakfast at 7:30 to dinner at 7:00, with a snack between every meal and one or two cappuccinos before 2 pm. Pick how you want to eat and the whole month rebuilds.

Cuisine days

No meal repeats within the month. Each cuisine has about a week of its own dishes, so one or two cuisines fill those days and the rest of the month mixes in other dishes. Pick more cuisines for more themed days.
